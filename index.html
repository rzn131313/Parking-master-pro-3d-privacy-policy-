1 Overview & Scope

RK Apps built the Parking Master 3D game (Android package ID: com.aistudio.parkingmaster.vkmqrx) as an ad-supported 3D car parking puzzle game. This page informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of information if you choose to install and play Parking Master 3D.

By installing or using Parking Master 3D, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

No Account Required: Parking Master 3D does not require sign-in, registration, or personal profile creation. RK Apps does not directly collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or microphone/camera recordings.

2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core puzzle gameplay and preserve your progress across sessions, Parking Master 3D saves the following game-related data strictly locally on your device using Android Room Database (parking_master_3d.db) and Jetpack DataStore (parking_master_settings):

  • Gameplay Progress: Unlocked levels (1–100 across 5 stages), completed level flags, best scores per level, star ratings (1–3 stars), total score, and last played level.
  • User Preferences: Sound Effects ON/OFF, Background Music ON/OFF, and Vibration Feedback ON/OFF settings.
  • Gameplay Utility Counters: Remaining hint count and local ad-display interval counters used to schedule level-clear ads fairly.

This local data never leaves your device to RK Apps' servers because RK Apps operates no backend server for this game.

3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Parking Master 3D integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display ads and keep the game free to play. Ads in the app appear in the following placements:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed at the bottom of the Home Screen (never shown during active puzzle solving).
  • Interstitial Ads: Displayed at defined stage/level completion milestones and when restarting a level.
  • Rewarded Ads (Optional): User-initiated ads that you may choose to watch to unlock extra hints or retry a level with a bonus score boost.

Data Collected by Google AdMob

When Google AdMob serves ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and diagnostic information, including:

  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID (AAID) or device identifiers used for ad personalization, frequency capping, and attribution.
  • Approximate Location: General country/city-level location inferred from IP address (the app does not request GPS/fine location permissions).
  • Device & Technical Information: Device model, Android OS version, language, screen resolution, and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completion events, and SDK crash/performance diagnostics.

All data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. For full details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob & Google Ads Help: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4 Device Permissions & Hardware Features

Parking Master 3D follows the principle of least privilege and only uses permissions strictly necessary for gameplay and advertising:

Permission / Feature Purpose in App User Control
android.permission.VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback when sliding cars, hitting blocked paths, or completing levels. Can be turned OFF anytime inside Settings → Vibration Feedback.
OpenGL ES 2.0 (Hardware Feature) Renders interactive 3D isometric parking lots, vehicles, lighting, and smooth camera animations on your device GPU. Local graphics rendering only; no data collected.

Not Requested: The app does not request access to Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Fine/Coarse GPS Location, Call Logs, SMS, or Broad External Storage.

7 Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

  • On-Device Progress: Your game progress, stars, scores, and audio settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Android Auto Backup: Parking Master 3D supports Android's standard backup mechanism (allowBackup), which may back up your local game database to your personal Google Drive backup depending on your Android device settings. RK Apps cannot view or access this backup.
  • How to Delete Your Data:
    1. Open Parking Master 3D → Settings → Reset All Progress to erase all saved levels, stars, and high scores immediately.
    2. Alternatively, go to Android Settings → Apps → Parking Master 3D → Storage → Clear Storage / Uninstall.
    3. To delete or reset ad-tracking identifiers, go to Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).

8 Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Depending on your region (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR, the UK, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights regarding personalized advertising and consent:

  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based advertising in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).
  • Consent Controls (EEA/UK/US): Where required by law, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent prompt allows you to manage your consent preferences for personalized vs. non-personalized ads.
  • Audio & Vibration Controls: Sound effects, background music, and vibration can be toggled off anytime inside the app's Settings screen.
Security Notice: While RK Apps does not maintain external user servers, we follow Android security best practices and rely on Google Play Services and HTTPS/TLS encryption for ad communications. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% immune to risk.
